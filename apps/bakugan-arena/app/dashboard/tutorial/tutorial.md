# Bakugan Arena – Official Tutorial

## 1. Game Overview

**Bakugan Arena** is a strategic turn-based battle game. You place Gate Cards, deploy Bakugans, and play Ability Cards to eliminate your opponent's Bakugans.

**Goal:** eliminate all 3 opposing Bakugans, or let the opponent's timer run out after Turn 0.

---

## 2. Deck vs Match Resources

The deck you build and the resources you can actually spend in a match are **not the same**.

### Legal deck composition

- Exactly **3 Bakugans** (usually one family)
- Between **3 and 6** normal Ability Cards, plus **0 to 3** exclusive abilities (at least 3 abilities total)
- Between **3 and 5** Gate Cards

### Limits during a match

- All 3 Bakugans remain available until eliminated
- Only **3 Ability Cards** can be used for the whole match (exclusives included)
- Only **3 Gate Cards** can be placed for the whole match, even if your deck has more

---

## 3. Turn 0 — Setup

Before regular play, both players must each place a first Gate Card.

- This is **mandatory**: both timers run in parallel
- If a timer hits 0 during Turn 0, the match ends in a **draw**
- Once both gates are down, the game enters the neutral phase

---

## 4. Victory Conditions

You win if:

- All **3 opposing Bakugans** are eliminated
- The **opponent's timer** reaches 0 after Turn 0

---

## 5. Draw Conditions

The match ends in a draw if:

- No more Gate Cards can be placed by either player (and no battle is in progress)
- All Bakugans from both players are eliminated at the same time
- A timer reaches 0 during **Turn 0**

---

## 6. Neutral Phase (No Battle)

Outside of battle, you usually get one main action per turn. Order and restrictions matter.

### Place a Gate Card

Can be done first. Does **not** end the turn: you can still deploy a Bakugan afterward.

### Deploy a Bakugan

Places a Bakugan on a gate. After deploying, you can no longer place a gate this turn. Landing on an opposing Bakugan starts a battle.

### Use an Ability Card

Immediately ends the turn. Costs one of your **3 ability uses** for the match.

### Change attribute (special cases)

Some Bakugans (e.g. Preyas) can change attribute once per presence on the board. This can retrigger synergies with an already-opened reactor.

---

## 7. Battle System

A battle starts as soon as two opposing Bakugans share the same Gate Card.

Each battle lasts **2 turns** of ability / gate opening.

### Initiative

The player who placed their Bakugan **last** (the attacker) acts first — **not** the first occupant.

On a battle turn, a player may:

- Use an Ability Card (if one is playable in that context)
- Open the Gate Card if they own it, and it is neither already open nor blocked

### Power comparison

At resolution, each player's total **G** on the slot is compared. Multiple Bakugans for the same player (Triple / Quartet) add their G together.

**Why attack?** Going second gives you initiative and often the first chance to open your own gate.

---

## 8. After Battle — Board Cleanup

Battles never leave the board frozen.

- **Losing** Bakugans are permanently eliminated
- **Winning** Bakugans return to hand (they do not stay on the gate)
- On a **power tie**, nobody is eliminated: all Bakugans on the slot return to hand
- The battle's Gate Card is **removed** and the slot is reset
- The winner usually takes initiative for the next phase

### Domain cleanup (Clear Domain)

If no living Bakugan is left to deploy (all are already on the board or eliminated) and no battle is active, the game automatically returns every remaining Bakugan to hand.

**Why?** To avoid a softlock: with no Bakugan in reserve, nobody could place, and the match would stall. This is **not** elimination — only recirculation.

---

## 9. Gate Card Types

### Reactors / Elementals

On open, they often grant a power bonus (e.g. +100 G) to Bakugans whose attribute matches.

### Character

Double (or heavily boost) the power of Bakugans from the matching family.

### Command

Special effects (movement, multi-battles, revive, etc.). Some activate automatically at battle end.

### Traps

Strong open effects (often direct elimination or a major swing). When a trap opens, the opponent may sometimes cancel it with a gate-cancel ability before the main effect applies.

You can only open **your own** gate, during battle, if it is not already open or blocked.

---

## 10. Ability Cards

The game filters abilities automatically: a card that cannot be used in the current context does not appear in your hand.

### Exclusive abilities

Tied to a specific Bakugan. Some are only playable in the neutral phase; others only if your Bakugan is (or is not) on the board. They also spend from the 3-use counter.

### Persistent abilities

Some abilities stay active after use and re-apply when the Bakugan is redeployed (until cancelled or the effect ends).

### Out-of-battle eliminations

Traps or abilities can eliminate a Bakugan without a G comparison. Protections (vs ability, vs gate, or both) can stop those effects.

### Filtering examples

- **Air Battle** — Move to another gate. Missing from hand if no other gate exists.
- **Marionette** — Control / move enemies. Missing if there is no valid target.

---

## 11. Strategic Notes

- Place gates to create (or avoid) favorable battles — Character and reactors completely change a trade
- Each ability is scarce (3 max): save them for the decisive swing
- Attacking second grants initiative: often better than sitting on an empty gate too early
- Filling the board with all your Bakugans can trigger domain cleanup or leave you with no answer
- Traps are strong but counterable

Victory comes from timing: positioning, gate opens, and ability-counter management.
